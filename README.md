# VIX-CHINA-roughness-and-multifractality

Abstract: 
China's official volatility index, iVIX, has not been published since February
2018, leaving a gap in the measurement of forward-looking domestic
volatility. To fill this gap, we construct a daily China VIX from Shanghai
Stock Exchange 50ETF options for 2015--2026. The index closely matches an
independent construction and published iVIX values, and China Financial
Futures Exchange (CFFEX) CSI~300 options provide an additional market check.
VIX changes are anti-persistent, with detrended-fluctuation exponents around
0.33--0.35, well below the no-memory value of 0.5. The result is robust in the
long 50ETF sample, although the shorter CSI~300 sample cannot reject an AR(1)
explanation. Multifractal analysis indicates that heavy tails, rather than
time dependence, drive most of the spectrum. The index also carries positive
average implied-minus-realised volatility spreads in both markets, but these
spreads do not provide stable short-horizon return predictability. Overall,
the paper contributes a validated domestic benchmark, evidence on the temporal
and distributional structure of implied volatility, and a clear boundary
between a VIX-specific scaling fingerprint and structural spot-volatility
roughness.
